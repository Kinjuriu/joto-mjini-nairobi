The ground in the centre was {{q2.gap_c|c}} hotter than under Karura's trees

  • {{q1.county_median_c|c}} half of the county's ground was hotter than this
  • {{q2.hot_areas_median_c|c}} city centre and Industrial Area
  • {{q2.cool_areas_median_c|c}} Karura Forest and the Arboretum
  • Land surface temperature, not air temperature. Clear mornings, January to March 2023 to 2026.

Where there are trees, the ground is cooler

  • {{q3.built_minus_tree_c|c}} hotter: mostly built-up ground against ground mostly under trees
  • {{q3.tree_band_0_10_mean_c|c}} under 10 % tree cover
  • {{q3.tree_band_90_100_mean_c|c}} over 90 % tree cover
  • An association. Dry grass and bare ground were as hot as built-up land in these months.

About {{q4.pop_in_hottest20|int}} people live on the hottest fifth of the ground

  • {{q7.lst_gap_c|c}} hotter ground for residents of the hottest fifth of wards than the coolest fifth
  • {{q7.hottest_fifth.tree_pct|pct}} tree cover in the hottest fifth of wards, against {{q7.coolest_fifth.tree_pct|pct}}
  • This measures exposure: where people live on hot ground with little tree cover.

Islands of cool: Karura Forest and the Arboretum
