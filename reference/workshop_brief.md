# Workshop brief

**Event:** Land, Food and Heat Resilience in Africa's Arid Regions: Workshop, Nairobi

**Hosts:** Earthna Center for a Sustainable Future (Qatar Foundation), in partnership with the Children's Investment Fund Foundation (CIFF).

**Date and place:** Thursday 26 November 2026, 8:30am to 4pm, Sankara Hotel, Nairobi. In person, one day.

**Who it is for:** practitioners working on urban heat resilience, dryland restoration, regenerative agriculture and school feeding in Africa.

**Application:** expression of interest form, reviewed on a rolling basis; applications close 20 November 2026. Places are limited. Participants pay their own travel and accommodation. The form asks whether the applicant can deliver a presentation about their work.

**Relevant theme for this project:** urban heat resilience and cooling.

**What this means for the story:**

- The audience works on heat and cooling, so the story must be accurate enough for specialists and clear enough for everyone else.
- They will want something usable: the ward explorer, and plain statements of what the data does and does not show.
- A ten-minute presentation is likely; the story and slides must work without a live internet connection as a fallback (keep static screenshots and the static figures).
- Do not use the hosts' names or logos in a way that implies they endorse or commissioned this work. Mention the workshop only as the occasion it was presented at.
